Before you apply

Questions people ask first.

Do I need business experience to start?
No. A lot of the brands we have helped started with no experience at all. Getting the brand, the labels and the first round of products right is exactly what the first part of the work is for.
What do I actually get?
Hands-on help shaping the brand and giving it a visual identity with a logo and product labels, putting your first round of products together, and building the storefront where they will sell.
Is this a course or a franchise?
Neither. It is hands-on work with Keenan Williams on building your brand and getting your first products ready to sell.
What if I am still just researching?
That is completely fine — pick that answer on the form. You will get the same reply either way, and nothing is locked in by applying.
Am I committing to anything by applying?
No. The questions are there so we can give you a useful reply, not to commit you to anything.
Will I hear back?
We reply to every application with an honest answer about whether we are the right fit for what you are building.
