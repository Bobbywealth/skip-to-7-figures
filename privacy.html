Privacy Policy

How Skip to 7 Figures LLC handles the information you give us through this website.

Who we are

This site is operated by Skip to 7 Figures LLC ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, or want to reach us about your information, email business@wolfpaqmarketing.com.

What we collect

When you complete the application form on this site we collect the information you type in:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your answers to the application questions (capital, timeline, experience, interest, and who is making the financial decision)
  • Marketing attribution details if you arrived through a tracked link, such as the source, campaign and referring site
  • Basic technical details — the kind of device and browser you used and a one-way hashed copy of your network address

We also count anonymous page views so we can tell how many people visited, and how far through the page they got. Those counts are not linked to you as an individual.

Why we collect it

So we can reply to your application, understand which applicants to prioritise, and see how the page is performing. That is all we use it for.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information to anyone.
  • We do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

How long we keep it

Applications are kept while they are active and for as long as we need the record of a business conversation. Old applications can be deleted automatically on a set schedule. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Deleting your information yourself

You can delete your own data any time — no email needed. When you applied you were given a delete code. Open our delete page, enter the email address you used and that code, and your application is removed straight away.

Lost the code? Email us from the same address and we will remove it for you. The code is required so that nobody else can delete your record just by knowing your email address.

Who else can see it

Our hosting and database providers process this information on our behalf so the site and the application inbox work. They are not allowed to use it for their own purposes. We will also disclose information if we are legally required to.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser may store standard technical data, and if you start an application we remember your progress in your own browser's local storage so you can come back to it. That stays on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Contact us and we will action it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of the page.
